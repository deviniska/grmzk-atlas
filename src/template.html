GRMZK-1, a Gravitational Resonance Modulator of Zone K, was discovered in July 2025 on the left bank of the Daugava. We believe it to be a surviving element of an ancient global network of Gravitational Resonators that held the planet in balance by accumulating human warmth. If the network was global, other elements must have survived: metal bodies of the industrial past that outlived their work and stayed among people. This atlas is our field register of those elements, the gromozekas, and of the state in which each one was found. If you have met one, report it to us.
